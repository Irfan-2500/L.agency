Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

  • All our products are digital. Nothing is shipped by post or courier, and there is no shipping charge.
  • Ready-made PPTs: after the payment is confirmed you get a download button on the screen, a link in your email, and the file in My PPTs. Online (Razorpay) payments are delivered instantly. UPI payments checked by hand are delivered within 30 minutes to 24 hours.
  • Custom PPTs: delivered by email / WhatsApp within the time agreed with you before the order, usually 1–5 days.
  • Didn't get your file? Contact us and we will send it again.
