FAQs

When will CBSE Board Exam 2027 start?

CBSE Class 10 and Class 12 Board Exams 2027 start on Tuesday, 16 February 2027. All papers begin at 10:30 AM.

When is the last exam of CBSE Class 10 in 2027?

The last Class 10 exam is on Tuesday, 9 March 2027. The main subjects end with Social Science on 8 March 2027.

When is the last exam of CBSE Class 12 in 2027?

The last Class 12 exam is on Friday, 9 April 2027.

When is the CBSE Class 10 Maths exam 2027?

Mathematics (Standard and Basic) is on Wednesday, 17 February 2027, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

When is the CBSE Class 12 Physics exam 2027?

Physics is the first Class 12 paper, on Tuesday, 16 February 2027, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

When was the CBSE Date Sheet 2027 released?

CBSE released the date sheets on 6 November 2026, 101 days before the exams start.
