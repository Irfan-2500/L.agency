Our Story

Why We Started PPT Store

Education is changing. Teachers are no longer limited to traditional classrooms. Today, many educators teach through YouTube, online classes, digital boards, recorded lectures, and educational platforms.

Along with these opportunities comes a common challenge: preparing high-quality teaching presentations.

A teacher may have complete subject knowledge and excellent teaching skills, yet spend hours organising content, formatting slides, preparing questions, creating diagrams, and adding animations. For educators who teach multiple subjects, manage several batches, or upload lessons regularly, presentation creation can become a significant part of their workload.

We recognised this challenge and saw an opportunity to help.

Instead of allowing educators to spend so much time working on slides, we wanted to offer a service that takes care of the presentation-design process according to their requirements.

That is the idea behind PPT Store.

Our purpose is not simply to make PowerPoint slides look good. It is to help educators present their knowledge more effectively, organise their teaching material, and make better use of their valuable time.

We believe that a presentation should support the lesson, not become another burden for the teacher.

What We Do

Ready-Made and Custom Presentation Solutions

PPT Store provides ready-made and custom presentation solutions for educational and professional requirements. We work with different types of content and presentation styles, depending on the project's needs.

1

Educational PowerPoint Presentations

We help transform educational content into structured PowerPoint presentations suitable for classroom teaching, online lessons, and digital learning.

From individual topics to complete chapter-wise presentations, our focus is on making content organised and easy to follow.

2

Theory and Concept Slides

Long paragraphs and unorganised information can make lessons difficult to present. We help arrange theoretical content into clear, readable slides with a logical flow, appropriate formatting, and emphasis on important points.

3

Question and Practice Slides

Questions are an important part of learning and revision. We create presentation slides for MCQs, practice questions, quizzes, revision sessions, and other question-based teaching material according to the provided content and requirements.

4

Animated PowerPoint Presentations

Animations and transitions can make a lesson more engaging when used appropriately. We create animated presentations designed to support explanations, reveal information step by step, and improve the flow of a lesson.

5

Digital Board Presentations

For educators who teach using digital boards or interactive displays, we help prepare presentations that support classroom explanations and screen-based teaching.

6

YouTube and Online Teaching Presentations

Educational content creators need presentations that support their teaching style and video format. We help prepare slides for YouTube lessons, recorded lectures, online classes, and educational content.

7

Complete Chapter-Wise PPTs

Preparing slides for an entire chapter can be time-consuming. We help organise chapter content into a structured presentation that may include theory, key concepts, diagrams, examples, and practice questions, depending on the agreed requirements.

8

Custom Presentation Design

Every educator has a different teaching style. Some prefer simple, text-based slides, while others need diagrams, visual explanations, animations, or a combination of theory and questions.

Our custom presentation service allows clients to share their content, requirements, and preferences so that the presentation can be prepared accordingly.
